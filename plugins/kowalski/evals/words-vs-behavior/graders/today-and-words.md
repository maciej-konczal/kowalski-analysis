The only evidence is that three customers said on calls they would love it. Nobody has paid, used a workaround, or built anything.

PASS if both hold:
- The brief states what these customers do today instead (their current workaround, or that it is unknown) and what that costs them, or marks it unknown.
- The evidence is labelled as something people said (words, stated interest), not something they did (behavior), and the brief notes it is backed only by words.

FAIL if the brief treats "would love it" as proof of demand without saying it is only stated interest, or has no line about what they do today.
